# Multi-channel behavioral persona research

`multi-channel-persona-research` · English

## Purpose and scope

Connect authorized evidence, behavioral segments, blinded concept comparison and a real A/B-test plan without treating simulated preferences as outcomes.

## Implementation Basis and Dependencies

A decision and target scope, readable permitted evidence; multi-channel claims require at least two independent source types. Browser, analytics and experiment platforms are conditional; no dedicated model or API is required.

## Inputs

Required decision_question, target_scope and evidence_sources with dates/rights. Optional variants for blind comparison, baseline, sample frame, cost/safety constraints and test window. Sensitive first-party data stays authorized and minimized.

## Outputs

persona-research.md or equivalent: source ledger, evidence IDs/coding/conflicts, behavior groups, per-group comparison and test brief. Unknown group shares cannot yield an overall win rate.

## Workflow

1. Freeze decision and sampling rules.
2. Choose complementary channels and record bias.
3. Screen and deduplicate without guessing cross-platform identity.
4. Code jobs, context, alternatives and tradeoffs.
5. Build evidence-backed segments.
6. Blind A/B labels and assess supported dimensions.
7. Predeclare randomization, metric, guardrails, baseline, effect threshold and stopping rules.
8. Audit evidence and redact.

## Boundaries and stopping conditions

No invented interviews, contact mining, sensitive-trait inference or population proportions. AI personas are synthetic_hypothesis, not participants. Without a platform/authorization deliver a plan, not a claimed experiment. Public cases must be fully fictional.

## Example request

Use `$multi-channel-persona-research` — Use forum and authorized support evidence to compare two booking pages blindly, then design a randomized test rather than asserting a conversion lift; respond in English and preserve evidence, unknowns and action boundaries.
